Bayanin Sirrin


Mallakace mai saukin aikatawa kuma yana da kyau sosai, shi muke kira da babu yawa sai zafi, sirrin nan yanada karfi wallahi saboda mun jarraba shi, ba sau daya ba ba sau biyu saboda haka a jarraba insha Allahu za'ji dadin shi, bi'izinillahi, yadda ake aikin shine ana karanta


1.Ya Wadudu (Kafa - 70,000 )


Amma duk bayan kafa 1000, Za'a karanta Qulhuwa har karshe kafa 41, Sai kace ko kice (Ya Allah, Ka mallakamun wane ko wance) sai kaci gaba da wuridinka, har ka gama adadin kafa dubu saba-in din amma fa ba lallai sai a lokaci daya zaka gama aikin ba, Zaka iya daukan Sati daya kana aikin, Amma kada ka wuce sati daya da kwana biyar, ma-ana kwana sha biyar kenan.



Wannan aikin ana yinshi ne na tsawon kwana 15 babu yankewa in bakayi koda na kwana daya bane to aiki ya dawo baya sai ka sake aikin daga farko, saboda haka ayi kokarin idan aka fara a kammalashi domin samun natijar aikin
